# Canvas submission text

**Demo:** https://wjaas.github.io/pocket-window/

**Source code:** https://github.com/Wjaas/pocket-window

**Description:** Pocket Window helps students use short gaps between classes. Add study tasks with an estimated duration, energy level, and optional due date. Enter the time and energy you have, and the app suggests a task that fits. A focus timer and progress summary help you follow through. Tasks are stored locally in the browser.

**How I used AI and tested it:** I used ChatGPT Codex to develop the idea, build the interface and task matching logic, and add tests. I tested the recommendation rules for time, energy, completion status, and due dates, and checked that the local page and script load successfully. I corrected a due date calculation after a failing test.
